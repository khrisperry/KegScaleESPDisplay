Ready for the next pour

Place the container below the sensor. When the tap is ready, tap and release the physical button or select Start pour. The tap closes at your saved fill level.

For manual top-off, hold the physical button for two seconds. Pouring continues until release and ignores all LiDAR readings and cup detection. Servo calibration, STOP and the maximum pour-time limit still apply; distance calibration and stable readings are not required for manual fill. A press during an active pour stops it; release before trying again.

Operation notes

No automatic foam settling or refill. A calibrated closed command is applied at startup before Wi-Fi connects. The servo needs power to close the tap.

Finish servo and distance calibration in Setup before pouring.

Servo calibration

Disconnect the linkage for initial tests. Pulse width determines position; TS90MD travel is nominally 270°. Verify actual limits before connecting the tap.

Tests return to the saved closed position after two seconds. Before calibration, the provisional closed position is 1500 µs; keep the linkage disconnected.

Fill level calibration

Keep the sensor fixed above the container and away from the beer stream. Capture the tray with no cup, then an empty cup/pitcher. Set the cutoff at the desired surface level using a reference target, then verify with water.

Required: cutoff < minimum starting distance < maximum cup distance < tray distance − 20 mm. A stable optical reading cannot guarantee cup presence; validate placement during testing.

Fault thresholds

These settings control reading stability and when a pour stops.

Wi-Fi

Choose a network and enter its password. These credentials are shared with the tap display. If the device is offline, use Wi-Fi Setup on its screen.

Foam measurement test

Record a normal pour and watch the foam settle. Recording only measures; use Run or the physical button to start/stop pouring. Normal pours keep the cutoff and sensor fault limits. Manual hold ignores LiDAR checks; its readings are still recorded.

  1. Start recording with the tap idle and the empty glass in place.
  2. Start a normal pour. After the tap closes, leave the glass still and mark Foam settling.
  3. Allow the foam to settle for as long as needed within the five-minute recording. Mark Cup removed immediately before lifting the glass; keep it out for a few seconds. Mark Cup returned if you replace it.
  4. Stop recording and download the CSV.

Download readings (.csv)

Up to 5 minutes or 3,000 readings, at the sensor's native rate. Invalid readings are retained. Positive distance change means the detected surface moved away from the sensor. Markers attach to the next reading. Starting a new test or restarting the device clears the previous recording; download it first. Stopping the recording does not stop a pour—use STOP / CLOSE TAP.

Last five pours

Every pour records measurements every 500 ms, plus its start and stop readings. Saved records include the settings used, servo commands, normal/manual mode and final stop reason. Download a CSV to share for troubleshooting.

Waiting for device.

Records survive restart and are replaced as newer pours finish. The current pour is saved after the tap closes. The serial monitor now logs servo commands, button actions, pour stops and errors only; use the foam test or recorded pours for detailed measurements. The separate Foam test records at the sensor's native rate for five minutes.

Firmware updates

Choose the touchpour_display.bin application file from your build folder. The tap stays closed during the update and restarts when finished. Wi-Fi and calibration are retained.

Use the application image, not merged-binary.bin or a full-flash image. Keep power connected until the device restarts.

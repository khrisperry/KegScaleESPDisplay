Use a full-charge reading only when you know the clock is fully charged. This button fills the field; Save applies it. Full must be at least 20 above empty. Live updates leave your edits intact.

Changes apply immediately and survive restart. The clock stays connected to Wi-Fi and MQTT. Hardware revisions use different mappings; do not deliberately drain the battery for calibration.

To change Wi-Fi or MQTT settings, hold the middle button for five seconds and join the clock's open setup network.
